HEDGEROW v44

Fire support

Under fire

Hedgerow

Tuesday 11 July 1944, Normandy. Your American rifle company must fight through the bocage, field by field, and take a village (Saint-Jean or La Haye: it changes each game), then hold it at the end of an hour, or destroy every German on the field. Each move is an hour; there is no time limit for now. A German garrison holds it; more Germans march up from beyond the river.

  • 🪖 Pieces: your company: three rifle platoons (three sections of four men each, moving together; a fourth platoon comes later), the captain, machine-gun and bazooka teams, and two tank sections: Shermans and Fireflies. 1st Platoon is elite.
  • 📋 One move each: every piece moves (acts) once an hour. A piece that has moved is greyed out until the next hour.
  • 🖱️ Picking a piece shows where it can go straight away: red-hatched fields are a Move & shoot (it moves there, then fires), the other marked fields a Charge. Click the field (where in it you want to stand) and that's the action. The menu by the piece has the rest.
  • 🎲 Actions (click a piece for its menu): Charge: up to three fields, without shooting, and through a field of your own pieces on the way, but not through a village or hamlet (rifle platoons and tanks fight hand to hand if Germans hold the field it ends in), Move & shoot (up to two fields, to a red-hatched field from where it will have someone to fire at, then fire needing one more; machine guns can't fire on the move: they Charge to a new spot, then fire), Shoot (full dice), and the captain's Rally: not his move, he rallies twice an hour if he doesn't move or shoot, once if he does (before or after), himself included. He can Charge, Move & shoot and Shoot too (one die). There is no plain move: to move without shooting, Charge.
  • 👁️ Who can shoot whom: up to three fields away along a line of sight over open fields and lanes; villages, hamlets and orchards in between block it (and orchards, like houses, are cover), and so does any hedge except the ones the shooter or the target lines (below). Beyond the next field (two fields for machine guns, tanks and guns), one more is needed on the dice. Men behind or beside a tank are in cover. Red targets show who a piece can hit, with the score its dice need.
  • 🌳 Lining a hedge: a piece stands either lining one hedge of its field or out in the open. Where you click in a field is where it goes: near a hedge to line it, the middle for the open. While you choose, a green bar shows the hedge it would line (cover). Across a hedge: a piece lining it can fire through; a piece in the open can only fire at an enemy who lines it, needing one more on the dice; two pieces in the open can't see each other; two both lining it fire at each other needing one more. To line another hedge of its own field, Charge and click that hedge in its own field (green): its move for the hour.
  • 🧍 One unit to a field: only hand-to-hand fighting brings both sides into the same field. MG and bazooka teams and the captain can also stand in a rifle platoon's field (up to three with it); they still move on their own.
  • 🏘️ Villages and hamlets: houses round a yard. Hard to hit in. Only a rifle platoon takes the village: when one can reach it, the village field shows blue hatching.
  • 🌊 The river can only be crossed at its two bridges.
  • 💣 Fire support: your 81 mm mortars and 105 mm artillery stand off the board behind your back line (left), the Germans' behind theirs (right), near the top. Their rounds fly from there, and the pile of rounds beside each shows how many shots it has left. Artillery also falls on the fields round the target, on your own pieces too (the target warns you) (the American mortars get one more every 2 hours, the artillery every 3).
  • 🔊 Sound: rifles, the American .30-cal and the German MG 42, bazookas, tank guns, mortar bombs and shells whistling in, men running, tanks driving, and a battle going on in the distance. The 🔊 button at the top turns it all off.
  • ✂️ Barbed wire closes some gaps between fields: only tanks get through it, rolling over and flattening it. Men must go round, or wait for a tank to flatten it.
  • 🌳 Hedges: men climb over a hedge bank as they move (they hop up over it on the table), which uses one more move (the hedge stays); tanks drive straight through, leaving a gap. Charging across a hedge at Germans behind it is allowed, but they fight back hard.
  • 🛡️ Tanks can't be hurt by rifles or machine guns: only bazookas, Panzerschrecks, anti-tank guns, other tanks and the artillery can knock them out, and infantry can't charge a field held only by tanks. A tank firing on men hits every piece on foot in that field (not the captain), like a mortar.
  • 🎲 Dice: every shot rolls six-sided dice by the target: 3 or more hits. One more is needed for each of: a target in cover (lining a hedge, in a village or hamlet), long range (beyond the next field), firing on the move, and the shooter being shaken; a 6 always hits. Elite troops need one less; the Panther's armour one more. Bazookas, Panzerschrecks and tank guns kill men with every hit. Every hit pins the target, and a coin toss says whether it wounds as well (a section, a team or a tank lost). A hit on a piece already pinned kills: two pins kill. The last three rolls stay in the target's field, with a tag saying who fired, the score needed and the hits: bright dice hit, dark ones missed.
  • 🟢 Who can move: a green ring under a piece means it can still move this hour; a piece that has moved is greyed out.
  • 😵 Shaken (pinned, red ! marker): a piece that is hit is shaken. It can still act, but it needs one more to hit and it can't charge. It recovers if it rests an hour without being hit; it can use its move to try to rally itself (half the time, elite troops more often); or the captain can rally it, up to three fields away. A second pin on a piece already pinned kills: a section, a team or a tank lost.
  • 🛡️ In the German hour: when one of your pieces is fired on you can take cover: its men get down where they are (they crouch on the table), and one more is needed to hit it for the rest of that hour. It misses its move next hour, unless the captain gets it up (Rally, up to three fields away).
  • 💥 Fire support: the mortars and the 105 mm battery fire on a field your troops can see. Only these run short of ammunition; a little comes up every few hours. Each fires once an hour.
  • 🚚 Reinforcements arrive on your west edge: more bazookas, 4th Platoon, a machine gun and replacements. The Germans have a pair of Panzer IVs and a pair of Panthers on the way.
  • 🧩 The table: 28 mm-style models: four men to a section base, three sections to a platoon; tanks in pairs. The men are bigger models (1:54) than the tanks (1:76), and the houses smaller (1:144), as on many wargame tables.

Click a piece on the table for its menu (one piece at a time), pick an action, then click a highlighted field. Enter ends the hour; Esc cancels.
