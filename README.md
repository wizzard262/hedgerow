# Hedgerow

Normandy, 11 July 1944. Your American battalion fights through the bocage, field by field, to take a village and hold it, in six moves.

- **Companies** of three platoons (four men a base) with their captains, machine-gun and bazooka teams, Shermans and Fireflies, against Fallschirmjäger, MG 42s, a PaK 40, Panzer IVs and Panthers
- **Command points**: each company earns points for about two thirds of its pieces an hour and can save them; each piece acts once an hour
- **Charge, move and shoot, shoot, rally**; every shot rolls dice on the table
- **Hedges** block shooting and slow everything; Shermans and Panzer IVs carry hedge cutters; **barbed wire** must be cut or flattened; the **river** is crossed only at its bridges
- **React in the German hour**: a piece fired on can spend a point to try to pull back and take half the hits
- Only the **mortars and guns** run short of ammunition; **reinforcements** arrive on both sides

Serve the folder (`python -m http.server 8768`) and open http://localhost:8768/. Needs a browser with WebGL.
